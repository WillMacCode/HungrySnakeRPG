Hungry Snake RPG transforms the familiar snake formula into a colorful, hazard-filled campaign. Guide your ghostly snake through 100 levels across ten themed worlds, from neon cities and industrial wastelands to the Wild West, ocean depths, Hellfire, and Heaven. Every world introduces new challenges, and every tenth level brings a boss encounter.

Each level has an objective to complete while you keep moving, collect apples, and avoid dangers. Depending on the world, you might dodge falling bombs and meteors, escape laser lanes, navigate shifting walls, survive electric floor hazards, or find your way through portals and mazes. Hazards come with visual warnings, so pay attention, plan your route, and keep enough room to react.

Boss fights change the pace: bosses move above the arena, launch attacks into it, and grow more dangerous as their health falls. You can damage them by collecting apples—but that isn’t the only way to win. Watch for a boss shot to glow gold, then press E or use the on-screen parry button to reflect it back at the boss. Timing your parries lets you fight back without relying on difficult apple routes.

Progress through the campaign to unlock new mechanics and skins. Skins have their own passive bonuses and timed abilities, activated with Q and balanced by cooldowns. Stock up on consumable power-ups for extra help: press Z, X, C, V, or B to use them. Your equipped skin stays visible in the game’s permanent ghost style—but ghostly looks don’t make your tail harmless, so watch your turns!

Track your integrity, earn rewards, and take on each world’s boss. Hungry Snake RPG combines classic arcade movement with campaign progression, reactive hazards, unlockable abilities, and boss battles where skill and timing matter.
